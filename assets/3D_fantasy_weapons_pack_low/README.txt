3D FANTASY WEAPONS PACK - LOW LOD
============================================================

25 fantasy melee weapon models in GLB format with PBR textures, generated
with polyy.ai.

These are the decimated LOD meshes: never-visible faces culled, quadric
decimation to ~25% of the surviving triangles (3835-11583), JPEG textures.

Two fidelities are published on the itch.io page:
  - 3D_fantasy_weapons_pack.zip      full-detail meshes (~50k triangles each)
  - 3D_fantasy_weapons_pack_low.zip  decimated LOD meshes (~4-12k triangles each)
Use the low set for distant LODs, mobile, or crowded scenes.

ENGINE NOTES
  Godot 4:  drop the .glb into the project; import as Scene (Y-up, metres).
  Unity:    import the .glb with glTFast or UnityGLTF.
  Unreal:   File > Import with the glTF importer.
  Every model is centred on the origin and about 1 unit along its longest
  axis, so scale once per weapon to fit your character rig.
